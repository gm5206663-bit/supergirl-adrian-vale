# Kryptonian physiology — baseline versus Kael's exception

## Selected-series baseline
Secondary television reference describes solar absorption associated with yellow-sun empowerment and red-sun suppression of ordinary Kryptonians, with strength, flight, resilience, energy projection and enhanced senses among the resulting capacities. Broad wiki lists combine events across seasons; they are starting references, not guarantees that every ability is equally available in every scene. [3](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Kryptonians)

## Kael-specific author rule
He is already extraordinarily powerful on Krypton under a red sun through his own Talent-supported development. Red-sun exposure therefore cannot automatically return him to the ordinary baseline. This does not automatically grant immunity to every weapon, substance or form of suppression.

## Distinctions to preserve
| Distinction | Writing consequence |
|---|---|
| Species potential / demonstrated skill | A familiar power category is not proof of identical training or precision |
| Unsealed capacity / sealed access | Human-level access does not redefine his true strength |
| Detection / interpretation | Hearing a signal does not explain its meaning |
| Durability / absolute immunity | Large resistance is not universal invulnerability |
| Adaptation / current knowledge | Ability to learn does not supply missing information |
| Intention / physiological response | A power architecture can affect temperament without becoming a separate will |

## Research reservations
Do not import unsupported claims of complete immortality, telepathic immunity, infinite stamina or no need for food simply because an aggregated article lists them. Define local applicability before it carries a plot. Exact vulnerability interactions must be grounded in his actual history and the threat mechanism; neither automatic defeat nor automatic immunity follows from a label.

## Author amendment — hybrid substrate
U09 establishes a Kryptonian father and Daxamite mother. The baseline above describes ordinary source Kryptonians, not Adrian’s entire physiology. His existing red-sun development and qualitative strength remain fixed; hybrid trait expression is original design. The source distinguishes a lead-based threat to Daxamites from kryptonite-based threats to Kryptonians. [2](https://www.themarysue.com/supergirl-recap-s2-ep22-season-finale/) [3](https://www.cbr.com/supergirl-kryptonite-device-graves/)

Do not infer either automatic susceptibility or automatic immunity to either substance. Trace the specific inherited pathway, developed adaptation and seal interaction before a plot relies on it. Royal ancestry is social history, not proof of superior biology.
