# Adrian and Kara — canon-grounded relationship study

**Research date:** 28 September 2026  
**Status:** research and proposed design, not an approved relationship change  
**Question:** what would a romantic relationship actually require, given Kara's characterization and Adrian's established history?

## 1. Correction to the earlier suggestion

“Close friends with mutual attraction, neither admitting it” was a generic romantic setup. It was not derived from the specific character evidence or a reconstructed pre-pilot history. Nor is an already-established romance an automatic result of Adrian being handsome and working nearby.

Adrian is an original character. Canon cannot supply a uniquely correct relationship with him; it supplies constraints and evidence from which to design one. The author is considering romance, not yet recorded as approving a specific duration, exclusivity, shared history or discovery sequence.

This study separates episode events, interpretation, and proposed fan-fiction changes. Later episodes are evidence for characterization, not experiences pilot-era Kara has already had.

## 2. Kara at the pilot: she is actively dating, not waiting indefinitely

In the pilot, Kara declines Winn's movie invitation because she has an online date. She meets James as CatCo's new art director; his Superman connection and their exchange are part of that introduction. Her evening date goes badly, and the aircraft news gives her the information that sends her to Alex's rescue. These are separate causes, not an indivisible scene that must survive any relationship change. [1](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Pilot_(Supergirl)) [2](https://comicbook.com/comicbook/news/supergirl-recap-with-spoilers-pilot/)

**Implication:** if Adrian and Kara have already begun a relationship before the pilot, copying the unrelated online date without explanation is not fidelity to Kara. It is preserving an outcome after changing its cause. Equally, long acquaintance alone does not establish that she would choose Adrian.

I did not verify an exact pre-pilot employment tenure for Kara in the checked material. A six-month, nine-month or multi-year overlap with Adrian would be our story design, not a researched fact.

## 3. Friendship, attractiveness and romance are different variables

In *Childish Things*, Kara comforts Winn about his father; he tries to kiss her, and she does not reciprocate. Emotional closeness and kindness are therefore not sufficient evidence of romantic attraction. [2](https://ew.com/recap/supergirl-season-1-episode-10/) [3](https://comicbook.com/comicbook/news/supergirl-recap-with-spoilers-childish-things/)

In *Strange Visitor from Another Planet*, Kara helps Cat reconnect with Adam; Adam asks Kara out. This supplies a different example: she can consider a new romantic possibility rather than remain permanently frozen in an unspoken workplace crush. It does not establish that she dates every attractive man. [4](https://ew.com/recap/supergirl-season-1-episode-11/)

The author's Talent master, §36, requires established attractiveness and charisma to have realistic social consequences, while preserving other people's preferences, discipline and agency. §45.4 expressly distinguishes communication/empathy from forcing love, attraction, loyalty or forgiveness.

**Implication:** Adrian's exceptional appearance and developed interpersonal capacities must not be ignored. They can change attention, opportunities, comfort and compatibility. They do not mathematically produce love or settle how Kara reacts to his past. The seal must not erase learned social competence, but it also does not grant a coercive aura.

## 4. His historical reaction to grief collides with a specific Kara scene

The most important contrast is not simply “hero meets dangerous man.” In *Childish Things*, Kara reassures Winn that he is not destined to become his father; her reassurance draws a distinction between suffering and responding to that suffering with destructive choices. The recaps explicitly describe her referring to how their lives changed without them becoming their violent relatives. [2](https://ew.com/recap/supergirl-season-1-episode-10/) [3](https://comicbook.com/comicbook/news/supergirl-recap-with-spoilers-childish-things/)

Adrian's locked history is different: after some Daxamites murdered his parents because of their mixed union, he destroyed a city on Krypton and killed its people (author amendment U09/U10). His adverse power development matters causally, but the author explicitly chose genuine culpability.

**Implication:** Kara may care about Adrian, recognize grief and still be deeply troubled by what he did. The relationship cannot become credible merely by having her say she understands because she also lost family. Neither instant forgiveness nor permanent inability to love him is proven by the source. Her response has to be written as hers.

## 5. The series allows rehabilitation, but the comparison has limits

In *Truth, Justice and the American Way*, Kara encounters a former Fort Rozz prisoner who has built a constructive life on Earth, opposes the Master Jailer's executions and reassesses the treatment of a captive. This is evidence against treating “former prisoner” as a permanent, all-encompassing moral category. [1](https://ew.com/recap/supergirl-season-1-episode-14/) [5](https://theworldgoespop.com/2016/02/22/supergirl-season-1-episode-14-truth-justice-and-the-american-way-recap/)

It is not proof that she would immediately forgive mass killing. The offenses and circumstances differ. Nor can pilot-era Kara borrow this later experience before its causes occur in our branch.

**Implication:** a romance is not automatically impossible because Adrian was imprisoned. But believing that he can have a present life, trusting him with others' safety, forgiving his past, and choosing him as a partner are separate judgments.

## 6. Krypton is lived memory, not just shared alien biology

The pilot establishes Kara's childhood on Krypton and separation from her family. *For the Girl Who Has Everything* later makes her attachment to that lost home central through the Black Mercy fantasy and the painful return to her Earth life. [2](https://comicbook.com/comicbook/news/supergirl-recap-with-spoilers-pilot/) [4](https://www.silverpetticoatreview.com/2016/02/11/supergirl-recap-girl-everything/)

**Implication:** once identities are known, another adult who remembers Krypton could mean language, cultural references, ordinary memories, disagreement and grief—not simply stronger protection. This potentially deep connection cannot operate as conscious shared-Kryptonian intimacy while each believes the other is human. Familiar habits might create questions, but each clue needs a source and a degree of certainty.

## 7. Her mother and his imprisonment create a real history question

Alura is a Kryptonian judicator, and *Stronger Together* includes a childhood scene where Kara asks about her mother's work on a criminal case. The source also connects Alura to Fort Rozz sentences. [1](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Alura_Zor-El) [2](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt4550416/characters/nm1128574/)

**Consequences to resolve:**
- When did Kael's catastrophe occur relative to Kara's childhood?
- Was she old enough to remember reports or discussion of it?
- Would she know a name, a case, a face, or none of those?
- Did Alura handle this particular case? That would be original story design, not a fact canon can verify about our OC.
- What might Kael know of Alura or her family?

Do not assert that Kara necessarily recognizes him, or that she necessarily cannot. A large event creates a plausible knowledge pathway, not automatic universal memory. These questions must be settled before claiming many years of intimate acquaintance without recognition.

## 8. The seal is not a complete identity explanation

The author established that Adrian's powers are sealed and that he initially does not know Kara is Kryptonian. That restricts his powered detection. It does not suppress Kara's abilities or erase either person's cultural memory.

The pilot depicts Kara using super-hearing, including during her date. This confirms that her senses remain relevant in ordinary life; it does not establish a universal ability to read species or identity from any heartbeat. [2](https://comicbook.com/comicbook/news/supergirl-recap-with-spoilers-pilot/)

The checked material does not establish an automatic “Kryptonian detector” by proximity. I therefore will not invent one. Conversely, no file should assume the device hides DNA, historical recognition, language, habits and every medical anomaly. Any unusual physical clue we actually use needs a defined mechanism and a believable interpretation.

**Important correction:** “He cannot sense her” does not logically entail “she cannot identify him.” The relationship history must test both directions separately.

The author locked **Adrian not knowing Kara's identity**. The author did not equally lock Kara's ignorance of his species. A one-sided suspicion or discovery is therefore a legitimate possibility to test, not something to exclude for a symmetrical secret-identity trope. If she had recognized him, we must then explain her actual reaction and whether she would reveal herself. We cannot invent endless silence solely to protect the opening. Recognition of species, recognition of Kael-Varr, and knowledge of the crime are three separate questions.

## 9. Honesty becomes more consequential as intimacy grows

In *Star-Crossed*, the revelation of Mon-El's identity and deception damages his relationship with Kara, and she ends it at that point. The recaps emphasize the withheld truth, not only whether she finds him attractive or whether he cares for her. [1](https://ew.com/recap/supergirl-season-2-episode-16/) [4](https://www.buddytv.com/supergirl-recap-does-mon-els-identity-end-his-relationship-with-kara/)

This later episode is characterization evidence, not a compulsory future breakup script. Adrian's facts are different and much more serious. Concealing an alien identity for safety, concealing a history of mass killing, and actively making false claims about that history are also different choices.

**Implication:** mutual secret identities do not automatically cancel the significance of every other concealment. A serious romance built on ignorance may contain sincere love and a major eventual trust conflict simultaneously. We should know what Adrian has actually said, what he withheld, and whether he intended to tell her, rather than manufacture a vague “dark secret.”

## 10. What must change if a romance already exists

| Original source cause | Changed condition | Required reassessment |
|---|---|---|
| Kara is available for an online date | She is dating Adrian | Remove or explain the date; establish her actual evening plan |
| James arrives in her current emotional situation | That situation now includes a partner/courtship | Keep his job and Superman connection; do not copy the romantic response automatically |
| Winn hopes for reciprocation | Kara's relationship may already be visible | Rebuild his information and choices without making him a stock jealous rival |
| Alex helps with an unfamiliar date | Alex may know the person Kara is seeing | Reassess the conversation; do not assume approval, hostility or a background investigation |
| Kara learns about Flight 237 at that venue | Her location/company have changed | Rebuild the information route, decision and rescue consequences |
| Adrian is away and learns later | He may be present with Kara | Give him his actual information and voluntary-release choice; do not freeze him to preserve the episode |

The source premises of the pilot date and aircraft rescue are established in the pilot references. The right-hand columns are counterfactual design requirements, not claims about canonical scenes. [1](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Pilot_(Supergirl)) [2](https://comicbook.com/comicbook/news/supergirl-recap-with-spoilers-pilot/)

## 11. Revised recommendation

Do not select an indefinite mutual crush simply to avoid changing the pilot. If the author wants a meaningful pre-pilot romance, a stronger candidate is **actual courtship already underway**, with concrete shared experiences and clear choices—not a relationship that begins emotionally only when the chapter starts.

That candidate still requires its history: length of workplace overlap, how attraction became personal interest, who initiated, what they know, and why prior identity clues have not resolved the secrets. These are design choices to construct, not canon facts to pretend to discover.

A worked scenario and its branching consequences are in `planning/PRE_PILOT_BUTTERFLY_ANALYSIS.md`. It remains proposed. No partner status, chronology, identity reveal or revised chapter has been accepted by this study.

## 12. Author amendment: mixed heritage and murdered parents
U09/U10 now establish a Kryptonian father from an influential house, a Daxamite mother from a collateral royal branch, parents murdered by some Daxamites over their union, and Adrian’s subsequent destruction of a city on Krypton. This replaces the accident premise used in the first version of this study.

*Welcome to Earth* depicts Kara unfairly judging Mon-El after learning he is Daxamite, against the backdrop of conflicting inherited accounts of Krypton/Daxam hostility. [1](https://www.denofgeek.com/tv/supergirl-season-2-episode-3-welcome-to-earth-review/) [2](http://comicboxcommentary.blogspot.com/2016/10/supergirl-episode-203-welcome-to-earth.html)

This changes the candidate relationship in three substantive ways:
1. **Shared home is not identical upbringing.** Adrian can remember Krypton while also belonging to a people Kara has been taught to distrust. She cannot automatically treat him as a perfectly uncomplicated representative of home.
2. **Prejudice may be tested before the source episode.** If she learns his ancestry earlier, her actual experience with him can alter what she later thinks about Daxamites. Do not reproduce her original Season 2 response unchanged by author fiat. Conversely, she has not automatically learned that lesson at the pilot merely because the audience saw it later.
3. **A prominent family and a destroyed Kryptonian city create more recognition routes.** Family names, society/political reporting, case records and survivors may matter. None automatically links the Earth alias to the case; childhood dates and degree of publicity still need to be set.

His parents’ murder, the attackers’ responsibility, his own city destruction, and the ancestry of each participant are separate facts. Neither inherited prejudice nor sympathy for the parents is a substitute for Kara assessing the actual history. No romance, reveal sequence, childhood acquaintance or royal conspiracy is approved by this amendment.
