## Technology rule
Describe the target interface, inputs, scope, operators and conditions. “Advanced” explains why investigation is worth doing; it does not prove a device can defeat any opponent. Likewise, Kael's strength is not a reason to dismiss Kryptonian engineering without analysis.

## Artifact custody
When an object matters, record owner, holder, location, condition, access requirements and the last accepted change. No spare seal, hidden escape pod, unlimited energy core or emergency cure exists merely because it would solve the next scene.
