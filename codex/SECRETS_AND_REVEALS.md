## Reveal protocol
Identify observer, prior belief, evidence, reliability, new belief, who is told and resulting behavior. Update KNOWLEDGE_LEDGER and relationship records only for established changes. A reveal may alter canon; do not reset memories simply to preserve the next episode.

## No artificial eternal secrecy
The seal prevents the specified powered detection. It does not permanently stop ordinary reasoning. If Adrian sees decisive evidence, let it mean something. If someone identifies him credibly, do not invent a new universal concealment field after the fact.

U15 selects the Vartox rescue for the consequential criminal-identity/power reveal. No romantic-confession schedule is selected. The timeline's source-side disclosures are reader reference, not promises about what Adrian learns.

## Additional distinctions after U09/U10
His Kryptonian ancestry, Daxamite ancestry, maternal royal affiliation, murdered parents, own offense and Earth alias are separate information objects. Disclosure of one does not transfer all the others. Rhea, Lar Gand and Mon-El have no assigned opening recognition of Adrian merely because his mother came from a collateral royal branch.
