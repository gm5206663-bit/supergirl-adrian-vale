# World codex — this television branch

## Continuity scope
The story starts in the Supergirl television pilot's world. The opening involves National City, CatCo, the Danvers family, Superman's public existence and institutions dealing with aliens. Details come from the selected television branch; comic-book profiles and other adaptations are not automatic supplements. [2](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Pilot_(Supergirl)) [4](https://www.superherohype.com/features/356903-supergirl-pilot-recap)

## Social layers
1. **Public:** employment, media accounts, visible rescue, rumor and personal interpretation.
2. **Professional:** company access, source verification, workplace authority and confidentiality.
3. **Institutional:** alien investigations, evidence and restricted operations.
4. **Private:** family relationships, identity, personal history and choices.
5. **Reader-side:** scenes shown from a valid viewpoint without transmitting their information to absent characters.

This layering is a writing model, not an invented canon organization.

## Local anomaly
Kael's red-sun development is an explicit author-created exception. Ordinary television Kryptonian solar behavior remains a separate baseline. Do not replace all species rules with his individual physiology. [3](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Kryptonians)

## Original additions
Adrian’s mixed heritage and the murder/city catastrophe are author-established original additions (U09/U10). Further family genealogy, his device’s proposed engineering and any new CatCo interactions are story design. The earlier Veyra district/accident proposal is superseded. They are not source canon. His true strength comes from the author, not a fan power-scaling calculation.

## Time and geography
National City locations need scene-level geography when travel matters. Avoid invented exact distances or commuting times. The timeline records broadcast order separately from proposed narrative order. Later crossovers and cosmological changes must be verified before use; metadata for all episodes is not a complete chronology of every off-series event.

## Research guard
Do not use arrow-fanon as television canon, Earth-D biographies for this Kara, comic martial-arts lists for automatic Kryptonian training, or wiki fan calculations as measured feats. Source uncertainty belongs in references, not disguised as certainty in narration.
