# Institutional knowledge and identity linkage

## Canon baseline
The secondary Fort Rozz reference reports that authorities obtained prison data after the crash and concealed the site. Do not start from an assumption that every convict's history vanished. [1](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Rozz_(prison))

## Working local record model
1. A **Kryptonian identity record** may describe Kael-Varr and his offense.
2. An **Earth employment/identity record** describes Adrian Vale.
3. An **identity link** connecting them is a separate evidentiary claim.
4. A **power estimate** in a prison record describes the observation conditions at that time, not an exhaustive omniscient current profile.

At the opening, no identity-link scene or exact record contents have been accepted. Proposed design: the alias is unlinked, rather than all authorities having impossible universal amnesia. Do not invent a deleted database, corrupt official or complete record as settled history without a receipt.

## Investigation stages
Lead → provenance check → corroboration → hypothesis → identity test → revised confidence → action. A database hit can be wrong or incomplete for a stated reason; a negative hit is not proof of innocence. A face, accent or device reading does not automatically disclose the Talent's master rules.

## Human consequences
Investigators can have legitimate safety concerns. Adrian can be genuinely culpable and still entitled to a coherent process. The story should not make every skeptical person stupid or abusive to manufacture sympathy. Record who has authority to know and act, not only who has a file.

## U09/U10 record implications
A murdered mixed-world elite couple and the subsequent destruction of a city on Krypton provide plausible public, judicial and diplomatic records. The extent of those records and who accessed them remain open. Royal kinship, a record of Kael-Varr and identification of Adrian Vale are separate claims. Do not solve concealment through universal lost records or solve recognition through universal omniscience.
