# Daxam's royal family — source context and Adrian's branch

**Scope:** Supergirl television continuity; research for the revised background. These source characters are not thereby present at CatCo or aware of Adrian's Earth identity.

## Source-side family

King **Lar Gand** and Queen **Rhea** are Mon-El's parents in the show's Daxam storyline. Their political and personal roles belong to the television account, not the identically named comic characters' histories. [1](https://supergirltv.net/characters/villains) [2](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Lar_Gand)

*Welcome to Earth* establishes competing Kryptonian/Daxamite accounts of their antagonism and shows Kara initially treating Mon-El through inherited prejudice. Those accounts are spoken viewpoints, not neutral permission to portray every person from either world alike. [1](https://www.denofgeek.com/tv/supergirl-season-2-episode-3-welcome-to-earth-review/) [2](http://comicboxcommentary.blogspot.com/2016/10/supergirl-episode-203-welcome-to-earth.html)

## Author-established original addition

Adrian's mother belongs to a **collateral Daxam royal branch**, and his father belongs to an influential Kryptonian house. Some Daxamites murdered the parents because of the mixed union. The city Adrian subsequently destroyed was on Krypton.

The exact genealogical connection to the show's royal family is not yet designed. A collateral branch is not an unrecorded sibling relationship with Mon-El, and it does not automatically give Adrian a recognized title or succession claim. Who held which office when Adrian's parents were alive also depends on the unassigned chronology.

## Individual relationship boundaries

- **Mon-El:** possible family connection, different experiences and possible later acquaintance. No established meeting, friendship, rivalry or recognition at this stage.
- **Rhea:** no established order to murder the parents, knowledge of their attackers, prior encounter with Adrian or opening knowledge of his Earth whereabouts. Do not import her later actions as proof that she committed this original crime.
- **Lar Gand:** no established sponsorship, protection, persecution or prior encounter with Adrian. A general royal ideology does not settle his response to an event we have not dated.

These are not claims that none of them could have known the family; they are unresolved questions. A later history decision can create such a relationship with a coherent chronology.

## Medical distinction

The Season 2 finale uses atmospheric lead against Daxamites; the same plot distinguishes this from an anti-Kryptonian kryptonite weapon. [2](https://www.themarysue.com/supergirl-recap-s2-ep22-season-finale/) [3](https://www.cbr.com/supergirl-kryptonite-device-graves/)

Adrian's inherited susceptibility, developed resistance and sealed-access behavior must be specified separately. Neither automatic lead vulnerability nor automatic immunity follows from merely writing “hybrid.” His existing author-established power is not reset. Royal status itself is not a new biological power source.
