# Evelyn Hart — original Earth support figure

**Human, 34, stage and screen actress.** Original character introduced under U31, not imported from any franchise, not a romantic partner.

## Appearance
Graceful, warm, expressive. Dark wavy hair often pulled back for rehearsals, clear eyes, soft smile. Dresses simply when off-stage: sweater, coat, scarf. Carries a small duffel with scripts and a chipped mug she refuses to replace. Beautiful in a lived-in, kind way rather than a posed publicity still.

## History with Adrian
Met Adrian about four years before the Pilot, shortly after he reached National City area following Fort Rozz and after building his self-built seal. He was in short-term housing in a building where she rented while doing a small theater run. She noticed he struggled with idioms, shopping, phones, leases, how to be ordinary while sealed and tired from calibrating the device.

She offered practical help: showed him how to shop, cook simple meals, use transit, fill forms, find a quiet apartment with privacy to work on his device, handle human small talk. She did not ask for his full history and he did not offer it. She knows he has unusual abilities and uses a band/device to live at human-level, knows he is from elsewhere and wants an ordinary life. She does NOT know his Kryptonian name Kael-Varr, his mass-killing culpability on Krypton, his Kryptonian house standing, Daxam royal collateral connection, Fort Rozz prisoner fear network, DEO custody details, Astra/Non, Vartox/Hellgrammite/Reactron/Knox/Lord testing.

She calls him "kid" or "Ade", checks that he eats, teases his seriousness, is protective in a mother/sister way. He trusts her and considers her family. She is important to him.

## Present
Filmed in Vancouver for three months, has a week between projects for Thanksgiving. Comes to National City to see Adrian, no entourage. Stays in a modest hotel near CatCo, not his apartment, to give him space. Knows he works for Cat Grant alongside Kara Danvers but has not met Kara before this visit.

- Human, no powers, no seal, no combat training.
- Not a DEO agent, not a journalist, not a scientist.
