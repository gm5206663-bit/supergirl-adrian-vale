# S01E09 Blood Bonds

The night air over Lord Technologies still smelled of scorched concrete.

Kara hit the parking lot hard enough to crack asphalt, boots skidding, cape snapping. Above, Non banked, supersonic, carrying Hank Henshaw under one arm like luggage.

Kara launched again, absorbing momentum, flight counter rotation, fists forward. Non met her mid-air, forearm to forearm, shockwave rippling out over the lot. They slammed into each other, lifted, slammed again, proper superpowered, no jokes, no one-liners, just weight and speed.

They came down together behind Lord Technologies. Hank broke free, ran toward Kara, blood on his lip.

Non grabbed him again, took hostage, flew off before Kara could get hands on him.

J'onn could have fought. Shapeshifting, flight, super-strength, telepathy, density control, but stayed human to keep promise to Jeremiah and to protect Alex and Kara, even when Non tried to get an alien with eyes on forehead to read Hank's mind. He let Non take him.

Adrian was already there, helping evacuate, because he had heard the alarm from CatCo. He did not fight Kryptonians head-on. Two Kryptonians plus prisoners, not fight he could win without revealing, and civilian identity protected, and also because this was Kara's family, not his.

He lifted a beam that had trapped a guard, set down gently, moved debris, lifted a car.

He caught Kara when Non hit her through Lord lab wall, one arm around waist, absorbing momentum, setting her down.

"You okay?" he asked, quiet so only she heard.

"I am okay," she said, and flew again.

"Alura knew I would come for family."
