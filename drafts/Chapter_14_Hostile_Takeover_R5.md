# Season 1, Episode 8 — Hostile Takeover

## Chapter 14 — Hostile Takeover

The DEO holding cell was cold.

Kara stood outside it, powers back, arm healed, and looked at her aunt.

Astra sat inside, wrists bound with cuffs that glowed faint Kryptonian script. On the table outside the cell, in a sealed case, was a dagger. Green glow. Kryptonite.

Two days ago, in the alley behind Lord Technologies, Astra had held that same dagger to Kara's throat. Kara had been surprised not because Astra had Kryptonite — she knew Lord had been collecting it since Reactron — but because Astra was holding it bare-handed and not burning. Kryptonians should burn.

Astra had said she shielded herself.

Three weeks earlier, after the freeway and the train, Cat Grant had named the masked helper at 7am editorial: The Wraith of National City. Headline. Photo blurred, flight, lifting.

Lord had offered a reward for info on Wraith, once. DEO had said outside jurisdiction, once. Winn had set a private monitor, tag WRAITH, tracking sightings, closed, no civilian disclosure.

Lord had not found Wraith. But he had found that Wraith's field bent light, that he absorbed momentum, that he lifted without breaking, that he was not Kryptonian in the usual way.

So Lord had started developing shielding. Not for Wraith — for what Wraith might be fighting. He had pulled Red Tornado's arm after Kara melted asphalt with a wide beam in the industrial yard, had studied the ionization, had kept the Kryptonite he had been stockpiling.

During the week when Adrian took Kara close to the sun, Lord's satellites had caught a spike. A private log, once.

Lord had combined Wraith field data, Red Tornado ionization, sun spike, and Kryptonite. He had made a lining. Thin, like the DEO's Kryptonite-lined walls that weakened Kara when she barged in to stop Lane torturing Astra, but wearable.

Astra had not built it. She had taken it. During the attack on Lord Technologies at end of last week, when Non and Commander Gor attacked and Lord fought with high-tech gadgets and DEO backup and Kara hit Non mid-flight supersonic absorbing momentum flight counter rotation lifting slamming and Adrian helped evacuate lifting a beam and a car and catching Kara through a lab wall, Non had grabbed more than Hank. He had grabbed a case.

Now that case was open in DEO lab, empty, and Astra's suit had a new sheen at the wrists, where cuffs glowed.

Kara understood that now, standing outside the cell.

"You let me capture you," Kara said.

Astra smiled, small. "You are learning."

Behind Kara, Alex and J'onn — still in Henshaw's form for now, because only Alex knew — watched through glass. Alex was concerned that Kara was not ready to kill Astra if it came to it. Kara had said her cousin does not kill.

Adrian stood further back. Evelyn's theater mask magnet was in his pocket, because he had taken it from his desk after Cat's emails were hacked and he wanted something ordinary to hold.

Cat's emails had been hacked.

Cat Grant, CEO, personal, embarrassing, Lois Lane nasty names, Idris Elba rejection, and worse — board threatening to remove her.

Kara had overheard Armstrong, chairman of the board, on the phone in his office above CatCo, saying he hired someone to hack Cat to have her removed.

She had told James and Winn. They had gone to Lucy for legal advice.

Winn had a plan to get access to Armstrong's computer files, not by hacking from outside, but by being inside CatCo, because Cat had asked Kara to go through her emails and look for anything that could be used against her.

Now, Astra spoke only to Kara.

"Your mother used you to lure me out."

Kara flinched. "What?"

"On Krypton, your mother was judge. She put me and Non in prison. We were trying to save Krypton. The core was being tapped, excessive, for resources. We tried to stop it. A guard died. Alura had to sentence us. But she knew we were right. And she used you, now, to lure me."

Kara went to the room with Alura's hologram, asked if it was true, frustrated when Alura could not give all information, because Alura was AI, not mother.

Later, Cat, in her office, wine on balcony, called Kara in.

Kara, glasses on, assistant, came, arm healed but still remembering being human.

Cat looked at her, long, laying out evidence.

"You disappear when Supergirl appears. You are late when Supergirl is busy. You have same coffee order as Supergirl's civilian clothes have same stitching. You were sick same day Supergirl was missing after earthquake. And you are terrible at lying, Keira."

Kara's heart beat faster.

Cat said, "Take off your glasses."

Kara did.

Cat stared, then smiled, not as Devil Wears Prada, as Cat who had been hero for city when Supergirl was human.

"About time you trusted me," Cat said.

Kara said, "Do not tell."

"I won't," Cat said. "But you will work for me wherever I go. Even if they fire me."

They did not fire her.

James, Lucy, Winn found proof of Armstrong's hacking, files, payment to hacker. Winn had gotten access, not by breaking, but by being Winn.

James told Winn he should tell Kara how he feels. Winn looked at Kara's desk, at Adrian's desk, at Kara and Adrian not quite looking at each other but aware where the other was, and said nothing.

Alex figured out Astra wanted Kara to defeat her. Wanted to be in DEO.

Why?

The answer came as alarm.

Astra's goons — Non and Commander Gor — attacked Lord Technologies again.

Lord, with high-tech gadgets, fought them off for a while, DEO backup, but even with Supergirl's eventual help, situation did not look great.

Kara flew, faster than before Human for a Day, because cells remember, and hit Non mid-flight, supersonic, absorbing momentum, flight counter rotation, lifting, slamming.

Adrian was already there, helping evacuate, not fighting Kryptonians head-on, because even stronger now, two Kryptonians plus Non was not a fight he could win without revealing, and civilian identity protected, and also because this was Kara's family, not his.

He moved debris, lifted a car that had trapped a security guard, set it down gently.

Non hit Kara, hard, sent her through Lord's lab wall.

Adrian caught her.

"You okay?" he asked, quiet, so only she could hear.

"I am okay," Kara said, and flew again.

Lord, bleeding, gadgets sparking, said, "About time."

Astra, from DEO cell, smiled, because plan working.

The night ended with city below, lights coming on after earthquake, and Cat's voice from downstairs: "Keira! Where is my coffee? And also, where is my son?"

Kara had learned Cat has another son she sends money to but hasn't spoken to in years, Adam.

Kara looked at Adrian, who had chipped mug in hand again, because he had gone back to CatCo to get it after DEO.

"Do you have family?" Kara asked, because Astra was family and mother used her to lure Astra and Cat had son she did not speak to.

Adrian looked at the theater mask magnet, at the chipped mug.

"I did," he said. "On Krypton. My father was from an influential house, my mother from a collateral royal branch. They were murdered. Some Daxamites, over their union. I destroyed a city. I was responsible. Caught because exhausted. Built device myself because wanted to be ordinary."

Kara heard it now, not as a file, as from him.

"I am sorry," Kara said.

"I am too," Adrian said. "But I have family now. Evelyn. You. Alex. Winn. James. Even Cat, in her way."

Kara smiled, powers humming, finger healed, cartoon bandage still in pocket, little dog with cape, because it reminded her she could bleed and kiss and be human for a day and be Supergirl again.

From DEO, Alex's voice private channel: "Kara, Astra is awake again. Wants only you."

Kara tapped earpiece. "Coming."

Adrian placed his mug back on his desk, next to the theater mask magnet, and went back to work.
