## Why Episodes 3,4,5,7,8,9 Have Only One Chapter?

- **S01E03 Fight or Flight (Ch6)**: Single villain Reactron, single arc, fits in 4771w naturally.
- **S01E04 Livewire (Ch8)**: Single villain Livewire, plus Cat mother, fits in 3971w.
- **S01E05 How Does She Do It? (Ch7)**: Single episode about Kara balancing, fits in 3738w.
- **S01E07 Human for a Day (Ch13)**: Single episode about power loss, fits in 3750w.
- **S01E08 Hostile Takeover (Ch14)**: Single episode about Lord hacking, fits in 3649w.
- **S01E09 Blood Bonds (Ch15)**: Single episode about Astra/Non family, fits in 3620w.

Per U35: Trying to fit entire episode into one chapter failed only when episode is too big (Red Faced, Childish Things, Strange Visitor). Those were split. Others are not too big, so one chapter is enough and not rushing, natural butterfly alive.

## Why Episode 4 is After Episode 5?

Broadcast order:
- S01E04 Livewire
- S01E05 How Does She Do It?

Our story order:
- Ch7 S01E05 How Does She Do It?
- Ch8 S01E04 Livewire

**Reason:** Story order, not broadcast order, per timeline/EPISODE_INDEX distinction checked (EPISODE: duplicate check). In How Does She Do It?, Kara learns to balance CatCo and Supergirl, learns to ask for help. In Livewire, she faces consequence of CatCo work (Leslie Willis, shock jock) and needs that balance. Placing Livewire after makes her growth natural, plus Adrian seen further back sealed, bullpen pen hearing, natural butterfly. This is intentional, not a mistake, and verified by EPISODE check.
