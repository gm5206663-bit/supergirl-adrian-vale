# Serial foundation — the story being built

## Premise and dramatic question
A Kryptonian–Daxamite hybrid who once used extraordinary power catastrophically now works inside a city about to celebrate another Kryptonian's first public act of rescue. He has made his physical life ordinary by engineering a seal; he has not made his history ordinary.

The central question is not whether Adrian can become strong enough. He already is extraordinarily powerful. It is what he chooses to do with capacity, knowledge and responsibility when remaining anonymous and participating in other people's lives stop being compatible.

## Three continuing strands — WORKING DESIGN
1. **The public city:** CatCo, public accounts of Supergirl, work and consequences. Adrian must actually perform a job, cooperate and face criticism. His employment is not a standing excuse to be present at every private meeting.
2. **The private person:** voluntary restraint, practical expertise, a family he lost and victims he cannot turn into a technical problem. Ordinary pleasure matters without proving either moral recovery or emotional emptiness.
3. **The institutional past:** Kryptonian records, Fort Rozz survivors and a society learning to distinguish an alien's identity from an individual's acts. Recognition requires an evidence chain.

These strands converge through action, not a mandatory secret leak at a predetermined chapter number.

## Thematic contrasts
- Kara begins choosing visibility; Adrian has deliberately chosen concealment.
- Having the ability to act differs from having the information required to act well.
- Restraint may be a responsible choice or an evasion; the scene must earn the judgment.
- Technology can restrain immense power without making either its inventor or the captive foolish.
- A genuinely culpable person may build a life; that does not erase victims or make everyone owe him forgiveness.

## Promise to the reader
An exceptionally capable protagonist, a living canon cast, earned relationships, coherent secrets, and consequences that remember earlier actions. No endless upgrade percentages, compulsory humiliation to preserve tension, or repetitive reminders that he could destroy everyone.

## Boundaries
No pairing, superhero alias, fixed ending, exact body count, universal immunity or unconditional no-kill vow is selected. The first accepted scene still begins at the pilot boundary. The existing chapter remains a draft. See planning/STORY_ARCS.md for options, not destiny.
