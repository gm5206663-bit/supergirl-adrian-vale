# Timeline authority — Supergirl branch

## Two clocks
Separate episode/source order from the OC’s historical chronology. Airdate is not automatically an in-world date. No exact OC birth or capture date has been established.

## OC history: order locked, dates open
Native Kryptonian–Daxamite birth → holder-specific red-sun development and adverse temperament → Daxamite attackers murder the parents over their union → destruction of a city on Krypton and mass killing during rage → technological capture → Fort Rozz imprisonment → arrival on Earth → self-built seal and chosen ordinary life → CatCo employment → pilot opening.

Do not infer exact durations or how he left Fort Rozz merely from a chapter filename. U15/U16 establish universal prisoner recognition/fear and personal complicated Astra/Non acquaintance; Alura sentenced him. Exact dates and conscious-custody intervals remain unassigned.

## Pilot order, checked at recap level
Krypton departure/backstory → Earth/Danvers upbringing → present CatCo and James introduction → Kara/Alex evening and date → Flight 237 rescue → aftermath and new public identity → Winn/costume/crime response → DEO revelation → Vartox confrontations → resolution and later reveal scenes. This is a planning index, not frame-accurate episode verification. [2](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Pilot_(Supergirl)) [4](https://www.superherohype.com/features/356903-supergirl-pilot-recap)

The researched summaries differ in some age/duration wording. Do not import an exact age or arrival year until checked against an authoritative episode reference. Whole-series chronology, crossover integration and later-season revelations are not yet researched for this project.
