2. Canon camera and character knowledge are different. Adrian’s absence does not ban Kara/Alex/DEO viewpoints.
3. Do not claim a source span is covered while silently omitting major beats; track missing, partial, outside-span and changed beats separately.
4. Preserve causal ownership unless an actual action changes the cause. Do not nerf Adrian to protect an outline.
5. Keep prose clear and original. Do not copy show dialogue wholesale, recreate an entire episode as a substitute, or fill narration with editorial rules.
6. Foundation data stays outside the story. No repeated power disclaimers in dialogue or narration.
7. Keep accepted chapter files distinct from drafts, and source chronology distinct from a proposed chapter plan.
8. Check state synchronization and exact file hashes; passing code does not establish prose quality or source truth.
