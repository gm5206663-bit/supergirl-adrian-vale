# Threats without arbitrary weakening

## Source-side pressures
The pilot introduces public rescue, alien enforcement and Vartox's hostility. Later first-season summaries include CatCo corporate pressure, the Master Jailer's pursuit of former prisoners, and Myriad. These are source conditions, not proof that Adrian personally encounters each event unchanged. ## Local consequence model — WORKING DESIGN
| Pressure | Why strength is not the entire answer | What the story must not fake |
|---|---|---|
| Cat's business judgment | She controls employment and has goals of her own | She is not hypnotized by beauty or automatically outwitted |
| Disclosure of past crimes | Evidence affects trust, law and victims' interests | A punch does not erase records or create forgiveness |
| An emergency | Time, location and reliable information matter | Do not blind him after establishing a functioning information route |
| A frightened colleague | The colleague retains agency | No instant trust because he protected them once |
| Institutional investigation | Identity linkage, jurisdiction and proof matter | No perfect scanner or perfect anti-scanner without a mechanism |
| Technological containment | Knowledge of a particular architecture can matter | Do not invent limitless countermeasures merely to beat him |
| Collateral risk | Precision and priorities matter, even with ample power | Do not make every action inexplicably destroy the city |

An opposing character needs an objective and means; they need not exceed Adrian's raw power. A weaker antagonist must not conveniently survive a direct confrontation Adrian is established to be able and willing to end. Conversely, strength does not supply missing facts.

## Intervention record
Whenever canon and the OC collide: what did he know, when did he know it, where was he, which access state was active, what did he choose, and what changed? If he declines to help despite adequate knowledge and ability, that is a character decision with consequences—not a protected episode outcome.
