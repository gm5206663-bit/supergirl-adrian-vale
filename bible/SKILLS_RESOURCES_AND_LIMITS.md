# Skills, resources and practical limits

## Established and implied
**Established:** he built his own sealing device. **Reasonable supporting design:** he understands its engineering, can diagnose ordinary faults, and retains this learning when sealed. **Not established:** mastery of every science, every language, every martial art or all Earth computer systems.

## Working skill portfolio
- Advanced experience with his own power regulation and relevant Kryptonian engineering.
- Practical electronics/tool use sufficient to maintain his device and learn compatible local equipment.
- Executive coordination, document analysis and discretion consistent with the assigned CatCo role.
- Ordinary communication and cultural learning developed through actual life on Earth.
- Combat history exists, but specific schools, teachers and named techniques remain unassigned.

An unspecified resource is not a permanent prohibition. Establish acquisition and consequences before using it. Do not create a spare ship, perfect database or hidden mentor solely to rescue a broken scene.

## Accounting after an event
Record an actual expense, repair, equipment transfer, injury or information gain when the story creates it. Do not add decorative depletion to every passive adaptation or invent a lack of ordinary resources purely to suppress competence.
