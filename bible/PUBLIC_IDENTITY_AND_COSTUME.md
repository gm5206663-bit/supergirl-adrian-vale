# Public identity and intervention costume

**Authority: U23, 29 September 2026.** This corrects a characterization error, not a newly discovered desire for privacy. Adrian has deliberately maintained his ordinary identity for years. He is competent enough to prepare for public intervention without appearing bare-faced in recognizable office clothes.

## Author-selected visual direction
A **complete Krrish-like suit, mask and associated gear**. This comparison exists only between author and writer. The resemblance is coincidental. There is **no Krrish character, film, inspiration, alias, crossover, lineage or imported power set in the story world**.

## Local physical execution
- All-black fitted top and trousers.
- Long, split black coat with a high collar; coat tails, not a separate cape.
- Sculpted angular black mask covering brow, eyes' surrounds, nose and upper cheeks, with swept points along the upper edge. Not a full helmet or an ordinary small domino mask.
- Black boots and gloves; overlapping glove/cuff covers the sealing band.
- No employee badge, civilian watch, visible office shirt, branding or borrowed superhero emblem.
- Prepared fitted case, practical closures, private changing and nonpublic departure/return route. Releasing the seal permits already-established speed to be used while changing; no magical instant-costume transformation is granted.

These specific practical details are local implementation of the author's complete-outfit direction, not invented quotations. No exact textile, armor rating, historic manufacturing date or past superhero career is established.

## Operational rule
Normal civilian life uses Adrian's ordinary appearance. **Powered public interventions use the complete disguise.** He does not exchange that privacy for convenient press attention, an employer subplot or antagonist exposition. Emergency help and intelligent preparation are compatible.

Use an ordinary contact route while sealed. Change/release privately; keep the band covered in public. Keep civilian names off open radio and public microphones. Do not change costume in front of a public camera or fly openly between a filmed rescue and an identifiable civilian desk.

A physical disguise is not supernatural immunity to inference. Conversely, do not force Cat or an enemy to identify him from a masked news clip merely to preserve the superseded plot. If later discovery is needed, it must have a genuine supported cause and respect subsequent author direction.

## Private knowledge is separate
The author-directed power-plant reveal to Kara/Vartox/DEO and Chapter 4's controlled disclosures remain adopted. Those limited witnesses do not amount to a worldwide civilian-identity reveal. Fort Rozz prisoners already know Kael-Varr; a familiar voice at close range, direct confirmation, or deliberate unmasking in a secured private confrontation can identify Kael without naming Adrian Vale or his employer.

The mask is not a reason to erase U15's fear/recognition history. U15 is not a supernatural ability to see through every disguise on television.

Chapter 5 R2's bare-faced harbor flight → Cat identification/employment investigation → Astra recognizes Kael on television is rejected under U23. Preserve that draft historically; do not adopt its events. Chapter 5 R3 replaces the dependent plot, not only the clothing description.
