## Specific author ruling
This holder developed extraordinary powers under Krypton’s red sun, before Fort Rozz. His particular power development adversely affected temperament. The ruling applies to him; ordinary Kryptonians do not all gain this route.

The master’s capacity for emotional development must not erase the author’s harmful-development history. Equally, this local history does not mean every Talent holder becomes violent. Record the tension explicitly: the precise physiological/resource/integration pathway is still an open local design, not proven canon or a universal amendment. Do not invent red kryptonite, possession or an evil Talent to explain it.

## Local architecture
- Direct domain: his mixed Kryptonian–Daxamite body, cognition, identity, acquired adaptations and real capacities.
- Interface: actual solar/environmental interactions and technology he operates.
- Authored derivative: his self-built sealing device; an external engineered system, not the Talent’s detachable container.
- Substrate and resource accounting: a viable mechanism must be written before exact energy/absorption feats. “Adaptation did it” alone is not a technical explanation.

## Seal interaction
Working interpretation, explicitly labelled: the Talent remains inseparable and can operate through genuinely free layers while voluntary restrictions suppress powered output. Its existence does not give him a secret unsealed sensory channel. Supporting an informed ongoing restraint is not a separate AI deciding to obey him. Exact conversion/storage behavior remains open.

## Evidence and growth
