# Self-built sealing device

## Author-established function
Kael-Varr built it. It seals and conceals his powers, leaving human-level accessible superpowers. He can voluntarily release it. At the opening, restricted senses mean he does not know Kara is Kryptonian.

## Working physical design
A narrow dark wrist band beneath his cuff. This form appeared in the assistant’s first draft; it is a design, not a separate author ruling. Exact materials, power supply and construction history remain open.

## Functional model for consistency
Engaged: powered output and extraordinary sensory access are suppressed. Memory, education, learned techniques and existing appearance are not erased. Released: prior acquired capacity is accessible; not a temporary new transformation gifted by the device. Re-engagement should not be presumed instant in every circumstance until a procedure is established.

The device is not the Talent’s container. Its working explanation is a self-engineered restriction of expression, not extraction of the underlying principle. The master allows suppression of particular layers without destroying the holder’s continuity.

## Things not silently granted
No automatic release, perfect immunity to all detection, hidden cosmic battery, remote mind control, permanent personality cure, arbitrary countdown or unavoidable rage on removal. Ordinary observation may still identify a face, voice, history or suspicious behavior. The seal blocks a power route, not all reasoning.

## Engineering questions before a device-centric scene
Which channels does it regulate? What fuels it? How does consent/release operate? What happens if damaged? What signatures remain? How does it account for stored energy? Those are OPEN, not grounds to fake a completed schematic. The opening office scenes do not need answers to every engineering question; a breakage or combat scene does.
