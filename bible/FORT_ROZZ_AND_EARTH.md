# Fort Rozz, capture and Earth life — D04

## Canon context
Fort Rozz is a Kryptonian maximum-security prison associated with the Phantom Zone. The television account connects its escape to Kara's pod and places its crash in Nevada; authorities later obtained prison data and concealed the site. This is secondary-source research, not a fresh episode viewing. [1](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Rozz_(prison))

## Capture — author lock, with a working implementation
The author explicitly established advanced Kryptonian technological capture. Working design: responders use an instrumented containment operation—observation, field restraint, physical isolation and transport—rather than one person overpowering him. The restraint must address his actual developed output; red illumination alone cannot be treated as a complete answer to someone already powerful under a red sun.

This is a class of mechanism, not a claim that a named canon weapon does exactly this. An operational flashback still needs a credible sequence. Do not solve it by arbitrarily deleting his powers, assigning universal cosmic nullifiers, or pretending the civilians were not really killed.

## Prison history
He is a genuine prisoner, not the secret commander of every other inmate. U15 establishes that all Fort Rozz prisoners know and greatly fear Kael-Varr. U16 establishes actual personal acquaintance with Astra and Non in prison and complicated ties. Alura sentenced him. Other specific interpersonal histories still require design; fear is not command authority. Confinement may preserve identity without granting experiential growth during perfect stasis; the Talent master distinguishes continuous existence from continuous process.

## Earth-arrival proposal
Working design: he is among the people who leave the crashed prison and later builds an alias and ordinary life. A circa-2003 arrival is a proposed alignment with common secondary chronology, not a user-supplied date. Exact date, elapsed imprisonment and biological/subjective aging remain open. The pilot presents the Fort Rozz connection through its institutional reveal. ## Work and cover
He learns practical Earth procedures, develops his self-built seal and establishes a route into CatCo. No mind-wiping of all records, magic citizenship or unlimited fortune is presumed. Existing technical skill can support learning without replacing the time needed to understand institutions and language. Work under Cat is an author lock; exact recruitment history is a scene-design matter.

## Records and recognition
A surviving prisoner record and an Earth alias are different information objects. The local default design is no established link between them at the opening—not a claim that no records exist. See INSTITUTIONAL_KNOWLEDGE and SECRETS_AND_REVEALS before a recognition scene.
