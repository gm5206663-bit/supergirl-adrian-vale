## Trait/state rule
Suppression is an access condition, not a reduction of accumulated development. On release, use his established capacity; do not artificially make Kara stronger to preserve an episode outcome. Conversely, unspecified abilities are neither confirmed powers nor proof of permanent inability.

## Required feat record
For each established feat: event, mode/seal state, environmental conditions, effect, scope, actual cost, information source and later consequence. Current numeric feat ledger is intentionally empty: the author supplied qualitative strength, not fabricated numbers.

## Tension
Information, choice, institutions, relationships, timing and collateral responsibility can matter. Do not invent fear, fatigue or incompetence to keep him absent. If he becomes aware of a preventable emergency, write his actual decision; do not treat “canon must happen” as a motive.

## U26 — balanced development and physique
Adrian is lean, exceptionally refined and harmoniously athletic, with visible natural definition—not an ordinary slim man, an oversized bodybuilder or a slow brute. His established enormous strength coexists with agility, speed, flexibility, precision and coordinated movement. Bulk is not the measure of his power. This does not assign numerical ratios or invent unrelated powers/immunities. The sealed state restricts powered expression; it does not erase his established appearance or learned coordination.
