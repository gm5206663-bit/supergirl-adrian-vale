# Device engineering — D02

**Status: WORKING DESIGN.** Author locks: built by Adrian; full human-level accessible superpowers while engaged; voluntary release; no initial powered detection of Kara's species.

## Architecture
A wearer-specific regulatory field addresses his developed power interfaces. It restricts the expression of the capabilities it was designed to control, rather than extracting the Talent or reducing his existence to a removable battery. The narrow wrist-band form is the existing assistant design; its apparent watch face is a cover, not an in-story game display.

Proposed subsystems: paired user interface, field regulator, power-path limiter, ordinary diagnostic circuit and manually operated release. There is no personality, adviser or moral decision-maker in the band.

## Operating states
1. **Engaged:** human-level accessible powered output and ordinary sensory reach. Old memories, learned techniques and existing attractive appearance are not deleted.
2. **Deliberate release:** Adrian uses a paired physical control sequence. It need not be a long ritual or a convenient weakness. No fixed delay has been established.
3. **Released:** his accumulated real capabilities are accessible. This is not a new transformation loaned by the device.
4. **Re-engagement:** a deliberate controlled transition. A safe procedure must be staged when it first matters; no sudden invented inability to reseal to force a fight.

## Energy handling
Proposed rule: limit excess intake/throughput to sustainable operating conditions rather than stockpiling an unlimited external reserve. Removing the device does not automatically unleash a city's worth of deferred energy. The Talent does not provide free fuel. Exact materials and energy capacities remain undesigned; no fake technical precision is claimed.

## Failure behavior — proposed, not a plot mandate
A lost display or ordinary interface fault should not automatically unleash power. Destruction of the entire regulator may remove suppression; it does not erase the underlying abilities. Do not assert it withstands every attack or always fails exactly when a scene needs drama. Define the actual fault, affected subsystem and consequence before using a failure scene.

## Detectability
Concealment of powered emissions is not concealment of DNA, historical records, face, speech, fingerprints or behavior. A scanner requires defined inputs and capability. A standard medical device cannot simply print “Adaptation Talent”; equally, the band is not an automatic defeat of every alien technology.

## Maintenance and ownership
He understands his own engineering and may maintain it. This does not make him omniscient about every Kryptonian or Earth technology. Tools and materials must exist. Keep a parts/repair record if an actual scene changes the device; no repairs or damage are accepted yet.
