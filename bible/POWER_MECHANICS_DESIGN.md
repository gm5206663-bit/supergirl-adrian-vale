# Power-mechanics design — D01

**Status: WORKING DESIGN, not verified DC biology or a new user lock.** The author-locked results are preserved: powers under a red sun, major pre-prison strength, and harmful temperament effects. This file supplies a coherent proposed mechanism rather than pretending those consequences are fully explained by the word “adaptation.”

## Red-sun development
The proposed local pathway is an altered solar-metabolic interface. His mixed Kryptonian–Daxamite physiology is the biological starting substrate; years of holder-specific adaptation develop a broader usable energy pathway and increasingly efficient storage, conversion and control. Red sunlight is an available input to his altered pathway, not a magical declaration of infinite energy. The body and supporting structures develop together. Food, rest, environmental exposure, practice and real recovery still provide inputs.

This does not change how ordinary Kryptonians function. It does not make Adrian dependent on finding a yellow sun before his powers can exist. Yellow-sun exposure can change resources and expression later without being his origin story.

## Harmful temperament development
Proposed mechanism: a developing power-regulation architecture became coupled to stress/arousal pathways. During strain, it reinforced rapid mobilization, salience of threats and difficulty de-escalating. It did not create a second mind, remove every choice or make anger the only source of power. There were ordinary decisions and relationships before the catastrophe; a person is more than a diagnostic label.

The parents are murdered by Daxamites opposing their mixed union. Loss and anger then interact with this already harmful development. His destruction of a city on Krypton and mass killing remain the locked consequence. Mixed ancestry itself is not the cause of the temperament injury. This design neither excuses it as remote control nor invents exact attack sequences before a flashback is planned.

## Present integration
Neither “permanently cured” nor “automatically murderous when unsealed” is established. Technical understanding can improve regulation; emotional experience and choices remain real. The seal restricts expression, not moral responsibility. Any present relapse or recovery must have evidence rather than be inserted to prevent a rescue.

## Capability topology
Strength, movement, durability, senses, energy projection and fine control are related but distinct. No single comparison gives exact values to all of them. The registry separates the author's broad strength benchmark from proposed specific abilities. A missing number does not demote him to Kara's level; a broad claim does not grant omniscience or universal resistance.

## Test of a new feat
Possibility → input → substrate → resources → construction → integration → observed result → remaining scope. Preserve previous confirmed feats under comparable conditions. Do not apply a one-exposure beginner rule to a technique he has already mastered over years.

## U09/U10 ancestry amendment
D01 remains a proposed mechanism, now for a hybrid holder. Neither royalty nor the new ancestry grants automatic extra abilities. Lead and kryptonite interactions, including any historically developed adaptations and behavior while sealed, need individual specification. No new vulnerability may silently negate the established power floor.

## U17 — precision heat vision
The author permits heat vision to heal or control Kara’s injury. Current Chapter 3 R3 executes the narrower option: highly localized thermal bleeding control, while unsealed, after securing Vartox. This is an established-capacity use, not an instant adaptation invented during treatment. Full regenerative repair, universal healing, perfect diagnostics and broad energy-spectrum manipulation are not implied. Future broader applications remain design questions, not permanent prohibitions.
