The previous unrelated-family-accident premise is superseded. His parents are murder victims; they are not accidentally killed by his powers. The subsequent city destruction is a separate act by Adrian. Do not reduce it to killing only the assassins.

## 2. What remains unchanged

His Adaptation Talent is holder-dependent. His own development already produced extraordinary power under a red sun before imprisonment and adversely affected his temperament. He remains far above the show's Kara and Superman when unsealed. Mixed ancestry neither cancels these established capabilities nor automatically supplies additional powers.

His adverse temperament came from that specific power development—not from an assertion that mixed ancestry is inherently unstable or that Daxamite blood is morally bad.

On Earth he wants ordinary life because he is exhausted by his former one. He made his own sealing device; its human-level access restriction and voluntary release remain intact. His CatCo employment, attractiveness and opening ignorance of Kara's species remain unchanged. No romance has been selected.

## 3. Canon context versus this story's additions

In *Welcome to Earth*, Kara and Mon-El describe hostile histories between Krypton and Daxam. Kara's account contrasts a democratic Krypton with a monarchical Daxam, while their disagreement exposes inherited prejudice and competing narratives. Her first response to learning Mon-El is Daxamite is unfairly suspicious. These are characterization and political-context sources, not proof that every assertion made by either character is unbiased history. [1](https://www.denofgeek.com/tv/supergirl-season-2-episode-3-welcome-to-earth-review/) [2](http://comicboxcommentary.blogspot.com/2016/10/supergirl-episode-203-welcome-to-earth.html)

The show's Daxamite royal family includes King Lar Gand, Queen Rhea and their son Mon-El. [3](https://supergirltv.net/characters/villains) [4](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Lar_Gand)

**Original branch additions:** Adrian, his mixed parentage, his father's influential house, his mother's collateral royal branch, the murders and the destroyed city. The show does not supply these events. It also does not establish a rule that such unions are always illegal or that hybrid children automatically inherit a crown.

An influential Kryptonian house supplies the high standing the author selected without inventing a Kryptonian monarchy. Daxam royal ancestry supplies family and political connections, not an automatic throne, succession right, fortune on Earth or control of surviving Daxamites.

## 4. Family implementation — working details, not further approvals

- **Daren Varr — father.** Retain the previously designed name. House Varr is an original influential Kryptonian house. A possible implementation is a family prominent in infrastructure engineering and civic leadership, with Daren a senior engineer rather than an ordinary technician. His exact office, resources and influence are not assigned.
- **Ilyra Varr — mother.** Retain the previously designed personal name and married-name styling provisionally. She is Daxamite and of a collateral royal branch. Her birth-house name, formal title and genealogy connecting that branch to the reigning line remain unassigned. Do not present “Varr” as an established Daxamite royal surname.
- **The marriage.** The parents formed a mixed Kryptonian–Daxamite family. Whether the marriage was public, recognized by either court, opposed by their relatives, or followed by exile/disinheritance is not yet established.
- **Neris, the earlier proposed sister.** That unapproved supporting-family design is retired from the active roster. This amendment establishes the murder of the parents, not the death of a sister. It does not establish that Adrian must be an only child.

No close kinship with Mon-El, personal relationship with Rhea or Lar Gand, or involvement of the ruling family in the murders has been selected.

## 5. Causal sequence and unresolved mechanics

Native mixed-heritage life → extraordinary red-sun development with adverse temperament effects → Daxamite attackers murder his parents over the union → rage-driven destruction of a Kryptonian city and mass killing → advanced Kryptonian technological capture → Fort Rozz → Earth life and voluntary sealing → CatCo employment → pilot opening.

The city and the attack on his parents are on **Krypton**, following the selected option. The precise site of the parents' murder, the city's name, attack sequence and elapsed time between murder and retaliation remain open. A same-city attack is a coherent candidate, not an additional recorded author choice.

Before dramatization, explain how the attackers reached the parents, why Adrian's existing capabilities did not prevent their deaths, when and how he learned what happened, and how the destruction spread beyond the perpetrators. Do not silently nerf him, grant the attackers equal power, or invent mind control to solve these questions.

Kryptonian authorities responding to a catastrophe on Krypton provide a coherent jurisdictional connection to his established capture. Specific agencies and detailed capture mechanisms remain open. U16 explicitly establishes Alura as Adrian’s sentencing judge; this is an author ruling, not an automatic assumption about every original character.

## 6. Political and personal consequences to develop

**Strong inference, not completed scene history:** the murder of members of two prominent families followed by city destruction is likely to leave substantial institutional and public records. The extent of publicity, surviving evidence and individual recognition still need dates and information routes. A famous catastrophe does not mean every later observer recognizes his Earth alias.

Do not turn “some Daxamites killed his parents” into collective guilt for an entire people, or make every resident of the destroyed Kryptonian city an assassin. The murder, Adrian's response and survivors' experiences remain distinct.

His mother's background makes any later Daxam contact personal. Whether he remembers Daxam fondly, resents a particular faction, rejects a title, or has a complicated attachment to his maternal family must be developed rather than prescribed by ancestry.
