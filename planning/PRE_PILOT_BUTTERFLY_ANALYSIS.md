## B. Worked romantic candidate

The following details are deliberately proposed so the relationship can be tested as a causal history:

1. **Meaningful overlap:** about nine months working in adjacent roles before the pilot. This duration is original design, not a sourced CatCo employment fact.
2. **Initial attention:** Kara notices his exceptional appearance. Adrian notices her attention, but neither attraction nor his social understanding supplies intimate knowledge. Her actual behavior must show whether interest becomes mutual.
3. **Compatibility shown through work:** a disagreement over a task demonstrates that he can respect her judgment without patronizing her. A separate occasion lets her see him accept responsibility for an ordinary error without shifting blame. Neither event excuses his historic crime; these are possible grounds for a present opinion based on limited knowledge.
4. **Explicit courtship:** one of them makes an unmistakable invitation rather than months of endlessly ambiguous banter. They have been going on actual dates for several weeks by the pilot. Whether exclusive is a separate decision; this candidate uses exclusivity to make the counterfactual consequences clear.
5. **Current impression:** Kara understands him as a private, capable man with bereavement in his past—not as a confessed mass killer. Adrian knows her warmth, ambition and frustrations, not her species. This is a proposal for what each has disclosed, not an approved account of earlier conversations.
6. **Serious unresolved fault line:** his affection can be sincere while the omitted crime remains consequential. We must decide whether he intends to tell her before deepening commitment. Do not leave this as a vague secret whose content changes whenever the plot needs it.

This is not “canon proves they are already dating.” It is a branch candidate consistent with Kara being willing to date, contingent on earned mutual interest and a credible information history. A shorter acquaintance or different evidence could lead elsewhere.

## C. Historical recognition test

A useful provisional history would place Kael's crime outside Kara's personal memory of current events, with no childhood acquaintance. She could have heard a historical case without knowing the face of the man now using an Earth alias. That is a design possibility, not an automatic consequence of imprisonment.

The worked candidate provisionally assumes neither has confirmed the other's species. This is not an author lock about Kara. If her memories or observations make that assumption implausible, test a one-sided suspicion/discovery instead and its consequences for whether she tells him. Do not use the band to erase her faculties.

Before adopting it, set relative dates and answer:
- Was the case public, and what information would a child in Kara's household encounter?
- Did Alura handle it, and did Kael know anything about her daughter?
- Does the alias cover only the name, or has a recognizable photograph/physical detail been established?
- Has Kara ever heard him use his Kryptonian name, language or a distinctive reference?

If we choose a history where Kara would credibly recognize him, the opening relationship must change accordingly. We do not invent blanket amnesia or a species-concealment function for the band to protect the romance.

## D. Pilot-evening counterfactual

The pilot's source chain is: online date → disappointing evening → aircraft report at that venue → recognition of Alex's flight → Kara's action. [1](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Pilot_(Supergirl)) [2](https://comicbook.com/comicbook/news/supergirl-recap-with-spoilers-pilot/)

With an existing exclusive relationship, the unrelated blind date should not simply remain. A worked alternative is an evening planned with Adrian. That creates choices:

### D1. Together when the emergency becomes known
- Establish the actual news/phone/visual route. Neither character receives knowledge because the episode index says the scene is due.
- Kara has a specific reason to react urgently: her sister's flight. Adrian may not know that before she tells him.
- Kara's reaction may reveal something to him; human-level sensory access does not prevent recognizing an extraordinary act he directly sees.
- Once Adrian has sufficient information, his ability to release the seal matters. He cannot be frozen into a passive witness just to reproduce the show.
- Whether Kara completes the rescue alone, he assists, or the outcome changes must follow timing and choices. Do not promise the original rescue ownership in advance.

### D2. Their evening is separate for an established reason
A pre-existing commitment could put them apart. Then Kara's rescue can still occur without his timely knowledge, but the reason must be real and recorded before the emergency. Avoid manufacturing a sudden errand or dead phone solely to protect the episode.

### D3. Courtship is not exclusive
The blind date might remain possible, but it changes the meaning of their prior relationship. Do not call them an established exclusive couple while preserving her unrelated date. If the author prefers this route, define it explicitly rather than letting contradiction masquerade as complexity.
