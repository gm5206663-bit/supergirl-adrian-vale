# Prospective story arcs — D06

**WORKING DESIGN.** These are modular directions, not approved endings or forced episode outcomes.

## A. Ordinary life becomes public work
Adrian’s employment creates legitimate involvement in a city reacting to Supergirl. Give him a task with stakes and a result. Cat’s decisions and Kara’s source conflict remain theirs. Exit condition: a real change in responsibility, trust or knowledge, not simply another witty conversation.

## B. The cost of remaining concealed
An event gives Adrian reliable information and a genuine choice about using his capacities. The seal is voluntary; it cannot become an invisible author-imposed lock whenever intervention would matter. Exit condition: the decision has a consequence someone remembers. A release is not mandatory if another effective action follows from the situation.

## C. A record meets a person
An institutional or survivor thread may connect Kael’s past with Adrian’s current identity. Require a causal chain, not a coincidental perfect dossier. Exit condition: someone revises their belief and takes an action. No automatic forgiveness, relationship collapse or faction recruitment is predetermined.

## D. Power without repeating the old catastrophe
A later conflict may demand full-capacity precision, cooperation or restraint. The character’s old instability must not be treated as a permanent random rage timer. Exit condition: an observable choice or skill consequence; not a status-panel upgrade detached from events.

## E. Relationships after truth
If someone learns his history, let their response reflect their own values and the evidence. A romance, if later selected, cannot function as absolution. Friendship, respect, distance, conflict and professional trust can evolve independently.

## Long-range restraint
Do not plan every season before the first accepted chapter. The episode index is context; story causes govern the branch. No new antagonist, stronger cosmic tier or miraculous cure is required simply because he is powerful.

## F. Two inheritances — after U09/U10
A future heritage thread can connect the parents’ mixed union, hostility from specific Daxamites, the destroyed Kryptonian city and Adrian’s decision to withdraw from his earlier life. His relationship to either culture is not prescribed by biology. Royal affiliation may complicate recognition or obligations only after exact kinship and chronology exist. This is prospective design, not a mandatory royal return, Rhea conspiracy or repeat of Mon-El’s plot.
