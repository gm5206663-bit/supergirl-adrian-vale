# Scene bank — original possibilities, not scheduled events

1. **The staff list:** Cat’s actual business pressure reaches Adrian’s desk. He must decide what his job permits and what it does not. Do not replace source consequences with unlimited secret money.
2. **Two useful skills:** Winn and Adrian solve different parts of a real technical/work problem. Neither becomes the other’s incompetent assistant.
3. **An ordinary disagreement:** Kara disputes a professional choice. Adrian cannot settle it by being handsome, older or stronger.
4. **Maintenance without spectacle:** He diagnoses an actual issue or checks a component of his self-built seal. No fabricated imminent explosion, HUD voice or compulsory rage flashback.
5. **News arrives too late:** Public information reaches him after a resolved event. Establish the timing, not a retrospective claim that he ignored people he knew were dying.
6. **News arrives in time:** A different event forces an actual decision. If he can help, his response changes something; do not use canon preservation as his motive.
7. **A surviving document:** A person encounters one incomplete record. Establish provenance, translation and identity linkage; the record is not an omniscient biography.
8. **An informed boundary:** A colleague asks a personal question and accepts or challenges the answer for their own reasons. Privacy is neither instant intimacy nor automatic suspicion.
9. **A memory with people in it:** If a Krypton memory is used, show someone’s ordinary goal rather than treating the family as a decorative explanation for his strength.
10. **The aftermath of a disclosure:** Focus on another character’s independent response. Do not narrate Adrian’s intentions as proof that others must trust him.

Use only scenes that advance the chosen chapter. This is not a checklist requiring every chapter to contain trauma, a technology demonstration, flirting and a rescue.
