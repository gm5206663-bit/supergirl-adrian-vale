# Master project bible — Supergirl / Adrian Vale

## 1. The story
A native-born male Kryptonian–Daxamite hybrid, Kael-Varr, lives in National City as Adrian Vale. He works directly for Cat Grant. His extraordinary power predates imprisonment in Fort Rozz; he is not a novice who becomes strong only after reaching a yellow sun. He wants an ordinary life because he is exhausted by his former one. The story begins with the television pilot, not a different DC continuity.

The author chose canon on-page, OC woven alongside. Canon characters retain motives, private viewpoints and causal accomplishments. A genuine change by Adrian must carry consequences rather than be erased to preserve the episode. Original prose and dialogue—not a transcript or a scene-by-scene substitute for watching the show—must carry the fan-fiction interpretation.

## 2. Established past — USER LOCKS
Adaptation Talent developed through his own mixed Kryptonian–Daxamite existence. His particular development under a red sun produced extraordinary powers and adversely affected his temperament. Some Daxamites murdered his parents because of their mixed union. His father came from an influential Kryptonian house; his mother came from a collateral branch of Daxam’s royal family. In rage he destroyed a city on Krypton and killed its people. The murders and his subsequent destruction are separate acts. Neither wrongful conviction nor possession replaces his genuine responsibility. U09/U10 supersede the unrelated-accident premise.

Advanced Kryptonian technology enabled his capture and confinement in Fort Rozz. Do not portray Kryptonian civilization as helpless merely because ordinary inhabitants lack yellow-sun powers. His captors’ mechanism remains to be designed and verified against the setting before a detailed arrest scene.

## 3. Identity — DELEGATED DESIGN
Earth name: Adrian Vale. Kryptonian name: Kael-Varr. Apparent age: twenty-nine. Black hair, blue-grey eyes, exceptionally handsome. These details were created by the assistant after the author delegated identity creation. Exact birth date, chronological age, family members and city name are not established.

Job implementation: executive operations aide to Cat Grant—special assignments, coordination and business follow-through. Kara retains her assistant position and responsibilities. Do not turn his title into unlimited authority over CatCo.

## 6. Character and consequences
He is tired of his former life; that does not establish either total remorse or total lack of remorse. Ordinary pleasures, work, relationships and choices may matter without reducing his past to a charming mystery. Victims remain people, not proof that he is impressive. Do not automatically make every release a psychotic episode or declare historical instability completely cured.

His attractiveness has human consequences, not mind control. No romance or endgame pairing is selected. Cat, Kara, Alex, Winn and James retain agency. No automatic rivalry between Adrian and every man near Kara.
