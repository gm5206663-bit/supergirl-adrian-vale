# Chapter 23: Solitude

*Episode: S01E15 - Solitude*

The apartment was quiet when Kara woke up.

The sun came through the window and fell across the floor. The city had been going on outside since before she was awake. The traffic moved in the same direction it always moved. The people walked the same streets.

She sat up. She looked at the kitchen table. Non was there, as he had been every morning since he came. His hands were on the surface. He was looking at the window.

---

"You are up," Non said.

"I'm up," Kara said. She got out of bed and went to the kitchen. She poured coffee and sat down across from him.

"You have been watching me," Non said.

"I've been watching you," Kara said. "I watch you the way I'm watching Marcus. I watch you with my eyes. I watch what you do. I watch what you don't do. I watch where you go and what you say and how you act."

"And what have you seen?"

"I've seen you sit at this table every morning. I've seen you wait. I've seen you not leave. I've seen you tell me things that are useful and not try to run."

"That is what I've been doing. I've been waiting. I've been telling you what I know. I've been not leaving. And that's what you needed to see."

"And I've seen it. But I've also seen something else."

"What else?"

"I've seen you look at the window. I've seen you look at the door. I've seen you look at the phone. You look at them the way a man looks at things he wants to use."

Non was quiet for a moment. "I look at the window because I want to go out. I look at the door because I want to leave. I look at the phone because I want to call someone. But I don't go out. I don't leave. I don't call. I stay here. Because you asked me to stay here. And because staying here is the right thing to do right now."

"And what happens when it's the right thing to do to leave?"

"Then I'll leave. But I won't leave until it's the right thing. And I won't leave without telling you. And I won't leave to go to Lord. And I won't leave to go to the DEO. I'll leave when you and Alex tell me to leave. And until then, I stay."

Kara drank her coffee. "You aren't a prisoner."

"I'm not a prisoner. I'm a guest who isn't allowed to leave. That is a different thing. A prisoner is locked in. A guest who isn't allowed to leave is asked to stay. And I'm staying because I'm asked to stay. And because staying is the right thing."

"And when it isn't the right thing?"

"And when it isn't the right thing, you'll tell me. Or Alex will tell me. Or I'll know it myself. And I'll go. But I'll go the way I came. I'll go to you first. I'll tell you what I'm doing and where I'm going. And I won't go to Lord. And I won't go to the DEO. I'll go to the place that's right. And I'll wait there until you need me again."

Kara set down the coffee. "I don't know if I can trust you."

Adrian was at the console in the bullpen, the paper open, the ghost code log running. The chipped mug on the corner of the monitor. The theater mask magnet beside it.

He'd been holding the pattern for three weeks. He'd keep holding it. The paper didn't change.

"I know. And I'm not asking you to trust me. I'm asking you to watch me. I'm asking you to watch me the way you watch Marcus. With your eyes. With the paper. And if I do something that isn't right, you'll see it. And if I don't, you'll see that too. And you'll know I'm telling the truth."

"And what if you're lying?"

"Then I'll make a mistake. Because men who lie make mistakes. They forget what they said. They change their story. They act in a way that doesn't match what they said. And you'll see it. Because you're watching. And you're watching with your eyes, not with the cameras. And a man can't hide from eyes."

Kara looked at Non. He was calm. He was ordinary. He was a Kryptonian general sitting at her kitchen table, telling her that he would stay until he was told to leave.

She didn't know if she could trust him. She knew that he hadn't tried to leave. She knew that he hadn't tried to call anyone. She knew that he had told her things that were useful and hadn't tried to run.

And she knew that she was watching him.

"Stay here," she said. "Don't leave. Don't call anyone. Don't go out. I'll come back. Alex will come back. And when the time is right, we'll tell you what to do."

"I'll stay here," Non said. "I'll wait. I won't leave. I won't call. I won't go out."

Kara stood up. "I'm going to the DEO."

"Go. Do the work. Come back when you've something."

Kara looked at the notebook on Alex's desk. The names. The times. The pattern. It was all there. It was all on paper. And the leak couldn't stop it.
