# Chapter 17: Childish Things Part 2

Kara sat next to him on the couch, not too close. The apartment was quiet except for the refrigerator humming. Outside, National City was cold, January cold, the kind that makes windows fog.

"Winn, your dad killing those people is similar to me losing all of Krypton," she said. Her voice was soft, the kind you use at 10 PM when you're both tired and honest. "Both of our lives ended that day. When I was thirteen and my parents put me in a ship and I watched my planet die and I was in the Phantom Zone for twenty-four years. When you were eleven and your dad mailed a bomb and your mom left."

Winn looked at her, eyes wet. "You are better than me. You and Adrian are better than me."

"No," Kara said. "I have a sister who tells me when I'm being an idiot. And friends who help."

Winn's hands were shaking. He had been holding it in since he was eleven, since his father said "Winn, my boy, my best creation" and then mailed a bomb that killed six people because his boss Dunholtz stole his designs.

"Winn?" Kara asked. His heartbeat was fast, 120 then 140. She could hear it now, after the sun. Her hearing was better since she flew to the sun, her cells remembered, overcompensated.

"I know you're with Adrian," he said all at once. The truth before the explosion, the thing his father never said before the bomb. "I have known since you came back from the roof in November with chapped lips and you smiled different. Since you and him started leaving together. Since the second toothbrush."

Kara's hand was on his shoulder, friend, not romantic.

"I bottle things up," he said, voice cracking, looking at his hands that were good with computers. "Like him. My dad bottled until bomb. I bottle until I cannot. I have loved you since before you were Supergirl. Since CatCo. Since you smiled at me when I fixed your computer. I know you are with Adrian and Adrian is my friend, he helped me, but I cannot bottle anymore."

He started to lean in.

Kara saw it coming. Heartbeat 160, breath catch, shoulders leaning forward. She had learned to hear these things since the sun, baseline higher, hearing Adrian's pen across the room from the bullpen.

She put her hand up, gentle but firm. "Winn, I'm with Adrian."

He stopped. He had been about to kiss her, knowing she was with Adrian, knowing Adrian was his friend who had helped him and closed the private monitor and texted hallway when his heartbeat was irregular and didn't come because he trusts them.

"I was going to kiss you," he said, standing, ashamed. "Knowing you are with Adrian. Knowing Adrian is my friend. I was going to do it anyway, because I'm done bottling, but I was going to do it wrong, like him."

Kara stood too. "Winn, I need you to go home. You need space. Not forever. Just for now. You need to figure out who you are when you are not my guy in the chair, when you are not in love with me, when you are not your father's son."

Winn nodded and left, closing the door soft, not slamming.

Kara sat on the couch and texted Adrian: Winn tried to kiss me. I stopped him before. He needs space. I am okay.

Adrian texted back: hallway. I trust you. I am here when you need.
