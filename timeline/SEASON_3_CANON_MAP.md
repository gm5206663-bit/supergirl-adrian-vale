# SEASON_3 — Complete Canon Map

**Episodes:** 23

## Season Summary

Season 3 focuses on Kara's struggle with vigilantism, her relationship with Mon-El, and the arrival of Morgan Edge and Reign (Samantha Arias). Cat becomes White House Press Secretary. Major arcs include: Kara quitting CatCo to focus on being Supergirl, Morgan Edge's waterfront development schemes, Reign/Sam Arias's dual identity, the Dominators' arrival in the crossover Crisis on Earth-X, Kara and Mon-El's relationship deepens, Lena and Kara's friendship, and the season ends with Kara discovering Reign's identity.
