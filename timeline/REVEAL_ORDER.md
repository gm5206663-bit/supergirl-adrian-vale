A character may infer earlier than source timing if genuine evidence changes the cause. “Canon says later” is not a reason to make him ignore decisive evidence. Conversely, intelligence is not permission to infer a complete secret from nothing.

The reveal ledger must record observed fact separately from hypothesis and certainty. No permanent romance/identity-reveal schedule is locked.
