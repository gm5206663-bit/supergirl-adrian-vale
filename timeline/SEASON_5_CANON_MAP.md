# SEASON_5 — Complete Canon Map

**Episodes:** 19

## Season Summary

Season 5 focuses on Kara finally telling Lena she is Supergirl, Andrea Rojas taking over CatCo, the arrival of Lex Luthor as the main antagonist, and the return of Leviathan. Major arcs include: Kara reveals her identity to Lena, Lena's trust issues, Andrea Rojas as CatCo CEO using tabloid journalism, Lex Luthor's manipulation, Kara's relationship with Lena becomes toxic, the 100th episode features returning characters, and the season ends with Lex Luthor's defeat.
