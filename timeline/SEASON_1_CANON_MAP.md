# SEASON_1 — Complete Canon Map

**Episodes:** 20

## Season Summary

Season 1 introduces Kara Danvers, a Kryptonian cousin of Superman who hides her powers while working as Cat Grant's assistant at CatCo. She eventually embraces her identity as Supergirl while juggling her civilian life, her relationship with James Olsen, and her DEO connections through her adoptive sister Alex Danvers. Major arcs include: Reactron/Maxwell Lord as the season villain, Cat Grant learning Kara is Supergirl (E8), Hank Henshaw/J'onn J'onzz revealed as Martian Manhunter (E9), Astra's arrival and Kara's conflict with her (E8-E15), Bizarro as the mirror-image antagonist (E12), Fortress of Solitude and Lucy Lane (E15), and the Myriad/Kryptonite radiation crisis (E19-E20).

## Key Characters

### Kara Danvers/Supergirl

Kryptonian, cousin of Superman, adopted by Danvers family, works at CatCo as Kara Danvers, superhero identity public from episode 1

### Alex Danvers

Kara's adoptive sister, DEO agent, initially in a relationship with Maxwell Lord, later with Maggie Sawyer

### Cat Grant

CEO of CatCo, media magnate, witty and layered, learns Kara is Supergirl in E8, serves as mentor figure

### James Olsen

CatCo photographer, love interest of Kara, later becomes Guardian vigilante (S2)

### Winn Schott

CatCo IT guy, Kara's friend, becomes DEO tech expert in S2

### J'onn J'onzz/Henshaw

DEO director, actually Martian Manhunter in disguise, reveals identity in E9

### Maxwell Lord

CEO of Lord Technologies, main antagonist of S1, secretly working with Reactron

### Astra

Kara's aunt, former Kryptonian general, seeks to use Myriad for domination, killed by Alex in E13

### Non

Astra's husband, Kryptonian general, survives until E20

### Bizarro

Mirror-image counterpart of Supergirl, created by Maxwell Lord, has reversed powers (E12)

### Lucy Lane

James's ex-girlfriend, DEO agent, becomes acting DEO director in E17

### Reactron

Metal-powered antagonist, works for Maxwell Lord, killed by Superman in E15
