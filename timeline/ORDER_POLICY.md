# Episode order and calendars — D05

## Verified scheduling issue
“How Does She Do It?” was originally scheduled ahead of “Livewire,” but the broadcast order was swapped following the November 2015 Paris attacks. The TVmaze metadata therefore lists Livewire as broadcast episode four and How Does She Do It? as five. ## Working narrative choice
Use the intended story order for that pair: How Does She Do It? before Livewire. Keep TVmaze broadcast indices unchanged in metadata and identify the title whenever numbering could be ambiguous. This is a working continuity choice, not a newly recorded author ruling. The pilot is unaffected.

## Calendar rules
- Airdate is catalog metadata, not the day the fictional event occurred.
- Episode order does not by itself specify how many in-world days elapsed.
- Earlier-established cause must precede its consequence even across an episode boundary.
- The OC’s biological age, apparent age, subjective time and elapsed calendar time are distinct.
- A future crossover requires checking relevant outside-series episodes; this package’s Supergirl episode index is not a complete Arrowverse chronology.
- No dated anniversary or exact detention length may be derived from an unsourced guess.
