# SEASON_4 — Complete Canon Map

**Episodes:** 22

## Season Summary

Season 4 deals with anti-alien hatred spreading across America after President Marsdin is outed as an alien. Kara faces Agent Liberty and the Children of Liberty. Lena creates a Lead dispersal device that harms Kara. Major arcs include: anti-alien sentiment as central theme, Nia Nal (Dreamer) introduced as CatCo reporter, Brainy at DEO, Kara and Lena's friendship reaches its breaking point, Lex Luthor's influence, Kara finally tells Lena she is Supergirl but Lena has already discovered it.
