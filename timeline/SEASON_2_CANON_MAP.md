# SEASON_2 — Complete Canon Map

**Episodes:** 22

## Season Summary

Season 2 moves to The CW and Vancouver. Kara meets Lena Luthor and Mon-El (Daxamite prince). Cat Grant leaves CatCo to become White House Press Secretary, Snapper Carr takes over. Major arcs include: Project Cadmus (Lillian Luthor) as anti-alien organization creating Metallo, Mon-El's arrival and relationship with Kara, Alex and Maggie Sawyer's romance, James becomes Guardian, J'onn and M'gann M'orzz (White Martian) relationship, Kara and Lena's friendship deepens, and the season ends with the decision to reveal Kara is Supergirl to Lena.

## Key Characters

### Mon_El

Daxamite prince, arrives in pod, becomes Kara's love interest, later revealed as prince of Daxam

### Lena Luthor

CEO of L-Corp, Lex's sister, Kara's friend, doesn't know Kara is Supergirl yet

### Lillian Luthor

Lena's adoptive mother, leader of Project Cadmus, anti-alien extremist

### Maggie Sawyer

National City police detective, Alex's love interest, realizes Alex is a lesbian

### M'gann M'orzz

Green Martian, later revealed as White Martian, J'onn's love interest

### Snapper Carr

CatCo's new boss after Cat leaves, tough editor who doesn't like Kara initially

### Metallo

Cyborg created by Cadmus from John Corben, has Kryptonite powers

### Scorcher

Infernian alien, attacks President Marsdin

### Veronica Sinclair/Roulette

Alien fight club operator

### Parasite

Alien parasite, drains powers of Kara and J'onn
