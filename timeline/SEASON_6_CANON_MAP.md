# SEASON_6 — Complete Canon Map

**Episodes:** 20

## Season Summary

Season 6 is the final season. Lex Luthor brainwashes half the planet with the Obsidian Lens. Kara is sent to the Phantom Zone where she meets her father Zor-El. Major arcs include: Lex as the final villain, Kara in the Phantom Zone, the fight against Lex's brainwashed followers, Lena's redemption, Kara and Lena's final confrontation, the series finale where Kara chooses her future, and the return of original cast members.
