# Canon, continuity and pacing audit
## Supergirl — Pilot → Stronger Together

**29 September 2026 · Audit, not a new chapter**

## Verdict

**The story needs a pacing and episode-boundary correction, not another full Pilot chapter.** Adrian's rescue, Vartox's survival, Alura's sentence and the complicated Astra/Non history are deliberate branch changes—not mistakes to undo.

Four delivered chapters total **15,248 words**. The interrupted fifth draft adds **3,218 words**, still without entering Episode 2. Its office, interview and disclosure scenes repeatedly revisit consequences already established in Chapter 4. There is useful character material, but not enough forward movement to justify keeping the episode open.

The remaining Pilot material can be bridged briefly: **James's cape/identity explanation and the practical arrangement for Kara to work with the DEO.** Adrian's ongoing inquiry does not have to finish before the episode changes. The main story of Chapter 5 must enter **Season 1, Episode 2 — Stronger Together**.

## 2. Episode 2: verified source spine

*Stronger Together* is S01E02. Its central problem is Kara's developing judgment and skill alongside the Hellgrammite/Astra threat—not simply another fight proving that she can lift something. The following is a factual outline, not a substitute screenplay. [5](https://arrow.fandom.com/wiki/Stronger_Together) [9](https://www.superherohype.com/features/357567-supergirl-episode-2-recap-and-preview-for-next-week)

1. **DEO flight tests.** Kara evades missiles; Winn calls about a harbor fire.
2. **Tanker mistake and public criticism.** Her attempted intervention worsens the situation and damages the tanker, spilling oil. Maxwell Lord appears in the public-response thread.
3. **Cat's interview pressure.** Cat wants James to use his Superman connection. Kara worries Cat will recognize her.
4. **Chemical-plant investigation.** A guard is attacked; the stinger prompts Kara's memory of Alura sentencing a Hellgrammite.
5. **Alex's training lesson.** Low-level kryptonite suppression lets Alex demonstrate that raw strength does not replace fighting skill.
6. **Smaller rescues and teamwork.** Cat advises starting smaller; Kara brings Winn and James together to help. Their independent contributions matter.
7. **Astra coerces Hellgrammite.** He is not simply an enthusiastic, interchangeable soldier. He wants food and concealment; she compels him to participate.
8. **Food, not just a weapon stockpile.** The DEO identifies his chlorine-based biology and DDT consumption, then arranges bait transport.
9. **Alex is abducted in the source.** The trap goes wrong, taking her to Astra's warehouse. Kara is informed and searches for her.
10. **Astra confrontation and separate fights.** Kara confronts her aunt; Alex defeats Hellgrammite with his own stinger. Hank intervenes with a kryptonite knife and Astra retreats.
11. **Different Alura technology.** The DEO reveals an interactive Alura program. This is not the same scene as the Pilot's fixed recording.
12. **Interview handoff.** Kara brings Cat and her car to a secluded interview location; the fuller interview belongs to the next episode's continuation.

Sequence and functional outline cross-checked against SuperHeroHype and Vulture. Their exact descriptions of Cat's final landing location differ, so the audit does not falsely certify a specific rooftop or hilltop. [9](https://www.superherohype.com/features/357567-supergirl-episode-2-recap-and-preview-for-next-week) [3](https://www.vulture.com/2015/11/supergirl-recap-season-1-episode-2.html)

The episode is framed roughly a week into Kara's public activity. That is not permission to insert a fresh week after each chapter or to treat broadcast dates as fictional calendar dates. [9](https://www.superherohype.com/features/357567-supergirl-episode-2-recap-and-preview-for-next-week)

## 3. What Adrian necessarily changes

### Vartox is alive
He is now an intelligence source and a security problem. His cell, weapon and statements may affect the next investigation. He does **not** automatically cooperate, know every plan, or truthfully identify every superior. In particular, his Astra allegation and the truck/communicator lead appeared only in the interrupted fifth draft. **Neither has happened in the accepted story or the delivered Chapter 4 candidate.**

### Kara already knows more about Astra
Chapter 4 gives her Adrian's account of prison acquaintance with Astra and Non. She still lacks confirmation of their present whereabouts and operations. Meeting Astra alive could shock her, but she must not act as though the possibility of survival or the prison connection has never been mentioned. In the source Episode 2, those revelations occur during their confrontation; our earlier disclosure deliberately changes that surprise. [3](https://www.vulture.com/2015/11/supergirl-recap-season-1-episode-2.html)

### All Fort Rozz prisoners know and fear Kael
U15 includes **Hellgrammite**, not just Vartox and the Kryptonian leaders. Recognition must change behavior if he sees Adrian. This does not mean he already knows Adrian's Earth name, location or friendship with Kara. Fear may cause flight, bargaining or concealment; it is not mind control or automatic allegiance.

### An informed Adrian changes the danger
His unsealed superiority cannot vanish so that Kara/Astra can have their original duel. If he learns of a kidnapping and can act, calculate his actual response. Conversely, a sealed Adrian does not receive private-frequency calls or remote battle dialogue. Location, communication and his own choices must be on-page—not a convenient unexplained absence.

### The original ambush is conditional
The DEO now has a living prisoner and knows that Kara's family may connect to other escapees. It cannot mechanically repeat every uninformed decision from the source. If Alex is still abducted, it needs a cause that survives this new information and the available backup. **The kidnapping, warehouse fight, knife wound and escapes are not mandatory outcomes.**

### Kara's independent story survives
She still has to learn judgment, collateral-risk awareness and trained movement; work with Winn, James and Alex; and decide what kind of public hero she will be. Adrian's strength does not transfer those skills or decisions to her. Her growth must not require nerfing him, nor must his presence reduce her to an audience.

### Cat and James require a revised secrecy argument
Our original passenger recording already prompted ordinary recognition and remains shared/copyable. The source's reassurance that Cat simply does not notice Kara cannot be treated as a guarantee in this branch. Cat may still lack confirmation, but she must be allowed to infer from evidence. James's friendship with Superman does not give him Adrian's biography. The source interview pressure and James's wish for an independent career remain useful motivations. [3](https://www.vulture.com/2015/11/supergirl-recap-season-1-episode-2.html)

## 5. Adrian and relationship audit

**Retain:** native male hybrid; influential Kryptonian paternal house/collateral Daxam royal maternal branch; parents murdered by some Daxamites over their union; his genuinely culpable city destruction on Krypton; extraordinary red-sun development and its separate temperament injury; technological capture; Alura's sentence; exhaustion motive; self-built voluntary seal; superior unsealed strength; real Astra/Non acquaintance; prisoner fear; food attentiveness.

**No blocking contradiction found in the latest rescue/wound-care execution:** the call is ordinary; unsealing is voluntary; Vartox is decisively outmatched; wound care follows custody transfer; Kara consents; Alex later removes the fragment. The full offense is explained after the rescue, not before it.

**Do not freeze a narrow scene into a permanent power ceiling.** U17 permitted heat-vision healing/control; the scene chose bleeding control. That does not establish unlimited regeneration, but neither is it an author prohibition on every broader future technique. The exotic blade's heat-sensitive source behavior also argues against careless high-output heating of retained fragments; the existing localized low-output treatment should not be rewritten as indiscriminate burning. [1](https://comicbook.com/comicbook/news/supergirl-recap-with-spoilers-pilot/)

**No automatic romance or absolution.** Kara's gratitude, attraction, family loyalty, hurt and moral judgment may coexist. Food and rescue are not payment for forgiveness. Winn need not become jealous or know the whole case; James need not become a rival; Cat retains professional agency. The Talent master explicitly forbids forced love, loyalty or forgiveness (§45.4).

**Open rather than secretly filled:** exact crime date/city/count, genealogy, biological/chronological age, detailed capture mechanism, immunity/resistance profiles and current long-term stability. Earlier power is not lowered by those unknowns.

**Small prose clarification worth a later tracked edit:** Chapter 2's “Neither did I” after Kara reacts to his ancestry can sound as though he did not know his own parentage. Intended meaning: he did not know about *her*. No accepted prose is silently changed in this audit.
